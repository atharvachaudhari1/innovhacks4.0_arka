Desk

Straight answers before you train.

Does any workout video go to the cloud?

No. Pose estimation runs on the phone. Gemini only receives anonymous numeric telemetry — reps, angles, and heart-rate averages — never frames or photos.

Will it lose me when the gym is crowded?

FitGuru scores torso prominence, isolates faint bystanders, and freezes rep state for about 1.5 seconds if someone crosses the frame so the count does not jump bodies.

What if I am offline?

Rep counting, form math, and native TTS keep working. ElevenLabs and Gemini pause until you are back on a network, then the next debrief resumes.

Do I need a Galaxy Watch?

No. Vision coaching stands alone. Wearables add live heart rate, calories, and zone context for Gold and Platinum plans.

Is there a login?

No account is required. Android can greet you from the device contact name. History stays in on-device storage.
